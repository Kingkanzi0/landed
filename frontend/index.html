3
Validators read LI.FIEach one fetches the transfer's status from LI.FI and judges every term against it. They must agree term by term.
4
Plain code settlesLI.FI says DONE and every term is met: the fee goes to the agent. Otherwise it stays, and the client can reclaim it.
